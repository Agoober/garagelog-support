Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026 · Last updated September 30, 2026

The short version

GarageLog doesn't collect any personal data. What you enter stays on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud account. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

GarageLog ("the app", "we", "our") is built on the principle that your vehicle records belong to you, and only to you. This policy explains exactly what data GarageLog handles and how.

1. Data we collect

None. GarageLog does not collect, transmit to us, sell, share, or rent any personal information. We don't operate any servers, and we have no way to see what you store in the app.

2. Where your data is stored

Everything you enter — vehicles, VINs and license plates, fill-ups, odometer readings, service history, maintenance schedules, expenses, documents, contacts, photos and notes — is stored:

  • On your device, in storage private to GarageLog and its widget, and
  • In your private iCloud account, only if you have GarageLog Pro and iCloud Sync turned on, so it syncs across your own Apple devices. This uses Apple's CloudKit private database, which is encrypted in transit and stored under your Apple Account. Only you can access it — we can't. You can turn sync off at any time in GarageLog's Settings.

3. Vehicle lookups with NHTSA

Two optional features contact the public APIs of the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) directly from your device over an encrypted connection:

  • Fill In From VIN sends the VIN you entered, only when you tap the button, to look up your vehicle's year, make and model.
  • Safety recalls (GarageLog Pro, United States only) send your vehicle's year, make and model — not its VIN — to check for recalls, at most once a week while you use the app.

These requests don't include your name, location, account or any identifier from GarageLog, and we never receive them or their results. NHTSA's handling of requests is covered by the U.S. Department of Transportation privacy policy. If you don't use these features, GarageLog makes no network requests other than Apple's own services (iCloud and the App Store).

4. Notifications

Maintenance, document and recall reminders are scheduled and delivered locally by iOS on your device. We don't operate a notification server and never see when or whether notifications are delivered. You can turn reminders off in GarageLog's Settings or in the iOS Settings app.

5. Photos, camera and files

GarageLog uses the photo picker, the camera and the file picker only when you choose to add a photo, scan a receipt or odometer, or import a file. Receipt and odometer scanning runs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Photos you add are resized and stored as described in section 2, and are never uploaded anywhere else.

6. Widgets and Siri

GarageLog's widgets and Siri shortcuts read your data on your device to show what's due or record an odometer reading. Nothing is sent to us.

7. Purchases

GarageLog Pro is sold through the App Store as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

8. Third-party SDKs

GarageLog contains no analytics frameworks, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting services, social-media SDKs, tracking pixels or fingerprinting. It uses only Apple's own frameworks.

9. Children

GarageLog is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

10. Your rights and deleting data

You're always in control. Delete a vehicle in the app to remove it and everything recorded for it, or delete the app. To remove data stored in iCloud, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › GarageLog. You can export your records as spreadsheet (CSV) files at any time from the Vehicle tab.

11. Support requests

If you contact support through our GitHub request form, what you write is posted publicly on GitHub and governed by GitHub's privacy statement. Please don't include personal information in your request.

12. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy in a material way, we'll update the dates above and show the new policy in the app.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, visit the GarageLog support page.
